Stage 1 - Research Questions

Main Research Question:
Do machine learning models for liver disease prediction perform differently across gender subgroups?

Detailed Research Questions:
1. Do Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost produce different predictive performance for male and female patients?
2. Which model achieves the strongest overall and subgroup-specific performance?
3. Are the most influential predictive features consistent across male and female patient groups?
4. Does subgroup evaluation reveal fairness issues that are not visible in aggregate metrics?
5. Are false negative rates or recall values materially worse for one gender subgroup, creating a possible healthcare risk?

Operational Questions for the Analysis:
1. What is the gender distribution in the dataset?
2. Is the target distribution balanced overall and within each gender subgroup?
3. How large are the performance gaps across gender for each modeling scenario?
4. Does removing the gender feature reduce or increase subgroup disparity?
5. Do separate gender-specific models behave differently from a single global model?
